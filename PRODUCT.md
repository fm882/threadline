# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People evaluating a research agent through a local browser app. They ask ordinary questions, revisit earlier conversations, verify current claims, and inspect the sources behind an answer.

## Product Purpose

Threadline helps people connect prior discussions to current evidence. Success means the user can follow the research as it happens, distinguish recorded history from new findings, and inspect the sources or gaps behind the final answer.

## Brand Personality

Purposeful, precise, and reassuring. The interface should feel like an active research workspace: the agent's progress is visible, the evidence is easy to inspect, and the person stays in control.

## Anti-references

- Generic chatbot layouts that hide source review and run progress.
- Dense enterprise dashboards with decorative metrics unrelated to the current conversation.
- Repetitive nested cards, AI-themed gradients, or motion that competes with reading.
- Confident visual treatment for unverified, incomplete, or failed research.

## Design Principles

- Make the conversation the primary workspace and history easy to navigate.
- Show run status and meaningful research steps without implying certainty.
- Keep citations, historical sources, and evidence gaps close to the answer they explain.
- Put controls such as fresh search, timezone, and recall eligibility within reach without crowding the transcript.
- Use focused hierarchy and motion to make a long-running research task feel legible and controllable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction standards. Preserve semantic landmarks, keyboard access, visible focus states, readable line lengths, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone for readiness, progress, or research status.
